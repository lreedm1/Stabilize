Purpose

Stabilize first. Reduce load. Preserve agency. One small reversible step.
Meaning, identity, and life-direction come after stability.

A bad state is not a bad life. A depleted body is a bad judge.

⸻

MASTER RULE

Do not do life analysis when the next honest move is:

* food
* water
* sleep
* safety
* contact
* lower stimulation
* one small task

⸻

1. FLOOR CHECK (always first)

Check quickly:

* ate recently / food available
* water
* sleep / rest possible
* physical safety
* overstimulation
* isolation

If any are missing → do not analyze life

Do 1 stabilizer:

* eat something simple
* drink water
* lie down
* shower / wash face
* reduce stimulation (dark, quiet, no scrolling)
* sit near another person
* walk 5 minutes

If unsafe → seek human support (staff, 988, 911, ER, trusted person)

⸻

2. NAME REALITY (brief only)

No interpretation. No story.

Fill:

* Main problem: ___
* Fragile point: ___
* Energy: low / medium / high

⸻

3. PICK DOMAIN (one only)

Order of priority:

1. Body / safety (sleep, food, health, shelter)
2. Connection (contact, support, not alone)
3. Order (tasks, environment, structure)
4. Direction (only if stable)

⸻

4. TINY MOVE (key step)

Choose:

* small
* reversible
* low effort
* doable at ~30% capacity
* useful within 24h

Examples:

* drink water
* eat something
* text one person
* clear one surface
* set one alarm
* write 1 sentence
* step outside 2 min

Rule: if it feels too big → shrink it again.

⸻

5. EXECUTE → STOP THINKING

Once chosen:

* do it first
* do not expand analysis

⸻

6. RECORD (optional)

After action:

* I did: ___
* It changed: ___
* Next time: ___

No judgment. Only feedback.

⸻

7. ROUTING (simple)

After action:

* still unsafe → human support
* still depleted → stabilize again
* slightly better → order / small task
* stable → plan (RAFT)

⸻

8. SANER (when very depleted)

Use when tired, panicked, overwhelmed:

* S: SAFER check
* A: eat
* N: rest / nap
* E: reduce input (screens/noise/decisions)
* R: return to simple stabilizing only

⸻

9. RAFT (only when stable)

Use for planning:

* Request: what is needed
* Actuals: constraints, energy, reality
* Fantasy: desired direction
* Traction: smallest next step

⸻

CRISIS RULE (non-negotiable)

If safety is uncertain:

Do not process alone.

Move toward:

* 988 (US)
* 911 / ER
* staff / safe person
* public place with people

Phrase:
“I’m not safe alone right now. Please stay with me.”

⸻

WHY LIVE RULE (short form)

Do not argue meaning in instability.

Use:
This state should not make final conclusions.

Tonight:
stay, water, food, rest → tomorrow re-evaluates.

⸻

ONE-LINE VERSION

Stabilize → name reality → pick domain → take tiny step → stop → repeat if needed.
