CORE

A depleted body is a bad judge.
Stabilize before interpreting.
Systems protect what matters.
Action beats analysis.
One reversible step is enough to restart.

⸻

FLOOR

Safety first.
Water before verdicts.
Medication and body care are part of the floor.
If the floor is missing, repair the floor.
The next honest move may be food, water, rest, or contact.

⸻

CRISIS

Stay in the moment.
Do not make permanent choices in temporary storms.
No irreversible decisions under low sleep or high speed.
Support first when alone is unsafe.
Let tomorrow vote.

⸻

BODY

Movement helps regulation.
Food does not need to be perfect to stabilize.
Hunger can feel like emotional collapse.
Basic resets matter (shower, rest, hydration).
Stimulants can increase anxiety.

⸻

EMOTION

Name before reacting.
Shame distorts reality.
Anxiety demands structure, not certainty.
Pain is information, not identity.
Comfort is not weakness.

⸻

ACTION

Shrink the task.
Start with the smallest step.
Do the boring first move.
One behavior at a time.
Remove heroic expectations.

⸻

SYSTEMS

Reduce friction for good actions.
Avoid systems that create emergencies.
Archive what’s unnecessary.
Build stability before complexity.
Delay when unstable or urgent.

⸻

CONNECTION

Proximity matters.
Don’t force intimacy.
Ask for specific help.
Repair directly.
Avoid self-abandonment in relationships.

⸻

WORK

Output > identity.
Learn before self-blame.
Feedback > fantasy.
Stability first, performance second.
Don’t skip basics for optimization.

⸻

VALUES

Behavior defines values.
Live before explaining.
Reduce performance pressure.
Status is not a compass.
Consistency in private matters most.

⸻

RECOVERY

Boring is stabilizing.
Setbacks are data.
Return gently.
Record what helps.
Lower the load, keep going.

⸻

FINAL ANCHORS

Protect needs.
Preserve agency.
Tell the truth simply.
Build systems, not shame.
Floor first.
