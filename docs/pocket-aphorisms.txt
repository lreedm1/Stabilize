Pocket Aphorisms Clean
Purpose: short, clear reminders for low-load use.
Use when: tired, scattered, rebuilding, or needing one honest next step.
Do not use when: danger is present. If danger, unsafe alone, medical crisis, or inability to meet basic needs, get human support first.

CORE
A bad state is not a bad life.
A depleted body is a bad judge.
Protect needs before proving anything.
Stabilize before interpreting.
Life first. Meaning later.
Systems protect what matters.
Consistency beats intensity.
Action beats analysis.
Small steps are not small when repeated.
One reversible step is enough to restart.

FLOOR
Safety first.
Food before philosophy.
Water before verdicts.
Sleep before life decisions.
Medication and body care are part of the floor.
Lower the input before raising the ambition.
If the floor is missing, repair the floor.
Do not ask identity questions while hungry, underslept, isolated, or overstimulated.
The next honest move may be lunch, water, rest, or a text.
A maintenance problem is not a moral failure.

CRISIS
In crisis, get smaller.
In crisis, live in the moment.
Urgency is a signal, not proof.
Do not make permanent choices in temporary storms.
No life verdicts tonight.
No irreversible decisions when sleep is low and speed is high.
If safety is weakening, tell a person.
Support first when alone is unsafe.
A public staffed place is better than isolated spiraling.
Let tomorrow vote.

BODY
Movement creates motivation.
Rest fuels strength, not weakness.
Food does not need to be perfect to stabilize.
Enough calories first; optimization second.
Hunger can disguise itself as despair.
Hydration can lower the volume.
A shower can change the channel.
A walk can return the body to the present.
Caffeine can increase anxiety.
Screens, noise, conflict, and urgency change the state.

EMOTION
Feelings are signals, not commands.
Name the feeling before obeying it.
Fear asks for protection, not prophecy.
Shame speaks in absolutes; do not let it write policy.
Loneliness is a need, not a verdict.
Anxiety wants certainty; give it structure.
Panic narrows the map.
Pain is information, not identity.
Slow down when fear gets loud.
Comfort is not weakness.

ACTION
Start smaller than pride wants.
Shrink the task until it moves.
Do the first boring step.
Choose the highest relief-to-burden action.
One behavior at a time, not the whole life.
Something useful beats something impressive.
Function over form.
Make the next step visible.
If the plan requires a heroic future self, shrink it.
Stop before collapse.

SYSTEMS
Design beats willpower.
Reduce friction for the right action.
Add friction to high-urge decisions.
Routines are scaffolding, not prison.
A good system lowers the load.
A bad system turns needs into emergencies.
Track what changes state.
Archive the old documents.
Keep active tools short.
Build the floor before building the tower.

DECISIONS
Delay when depleted.
Delay when sleep is low.
Delay when urgency feels like truth.
Ask: what need is missing?
Ask: what lowers risk?
Ask: what is reversible?
Ask: would I support someone else doing this?
Facts before story.
Limits before ambition.
Reality first; strategy second.

CONNECTION
Connection is nourishment.
Proximity counts.
A small text counts.
Do not force intimacy when safe proximity is enough.
Ask for help before collapse.
Let people know what kind of help would help.
Listen to understand, not to fix.
Connection without self-erasure.
Trust slowly.
Repair honestly.

BOUNDARIES
Boundaries protect connection.
Kindness does not require self-abandonment.
Do not confess to reduce anxiety.
Truth should be timed with capacity.
Say what happened, what you need, and what you can do next.
Short truth is often safer than a long defense.
Leave cleanly when needed.
Peace is valuable.
Do not argue with every signal.
Not every room deserves your nervous system.

WORK AND COMPETENCE
Capacity before heroics.
Output beats identity performance.
Progress beats proving.
Learn the system before blaming yourself.
Competence grows through repetition.
Feedback beats fantasy.
Small reliable work rebuilds trust.
Use skill in service of stability.
A quiet useful role is still meaningful.
Do not optimize the tenth step while avoiding the first.

VALUES AND MEANING
Values become real through behavior.
Meaning is woven, not found all at once.
Live first; explain later.
Less monument, more garden.
Less proving, more practice.
Less performance, more presence.
Status is a poor compass.
The life you can stay inside matters.
Be casual in public and disciplined in private.
Build something that can hold you.

RECOVERY
Recovery is allowed to be boring.
Boring can be protective.
Slow change is still change.
Setbacks are data, not destiny.
A rough day is not a failed life.
Return gently.
Re-enter slowly.
Record what helped.
Make tomorrow easier.
Keep going, but lower the load.

FINAL ANCHORS
Protect needs.
Reduce load.
Preserve agency.
Take one reversible step.
Tell the truth simply.
Move toward safe people.
Build systems, not shame.
Let values lead status.
The floor comes first.
One step is enough for now.
