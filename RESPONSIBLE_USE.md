# Responsible use

Stabilize is intended to reduce preventable harm by protecting basic needs, lowering cognitive load, and preserving user agency.

Do not use this project or a derivative to:

- encourage, facilitate, optimize, or conceal self-harm or violence
- exploit a person's age, disability, distress, isolation, medical condition, or other vulnerability
- impersonate a clinician, crisis service, emergency service, or trusted person
- make unsafe diagnosis, medication, or treatment claims
- replace emergency routing with engagement or retention goals
- coerce, shame, surveil, rank, punish, or discriminate against people using inferred health or emotional data
- collect, sell, expose, or repurpose sensitive conversations without specific, informed permission
- falsely claim that an AI system can guarantee safety, confidentiality, clinical benefit, or professional care

Deployers should keep urgent routes outside the generative model, test changes adversarially, minimize data, treat pseudonymous session and network aliases as sensitive, provide an effective deletion control, make AI generation clear to users, provide a direct path to human help, and publish limitations in plain language.
