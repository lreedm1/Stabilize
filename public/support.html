Support

Get help with Stabilize

For a bug, access problem, privacy question, or App Store support request, email lreedm1@gmail.com.

Include only what is needed

Describe the device, operating-system version, what you expected, what happened, and any error reference shown. Do not email passwords, authentication codes, financial account numbers, medical records, or a private chat transcript.

This inbox is not emergency support

Support email is not continuously monitored and cannot provide crisis response, medical care, or guaranteed response times. In the United States, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. For immediate danger, a possible overdose, serious injury, or a medical emergency, call 911 or go to an emergency department.
