Stabilize support

How can we help?

Email info@reedlokken.com for help with the Stabilize iOS app or website.

What support can help with

  • Installation, launch, connection, display, or accessibility problems.
  • Unexpected app behavior, broken links, or a response that did not load.
  • Questions about app features, privacy, or AI processing.
  • Privacy and deletion requests for data that Stabilize controls.

For a technical problem, include your device model, iOS or browser version, what you expected, and what happened. You may include a screenshot only after removing private information.

Please do not email chat text or sensitive information. Do not send your prompts, AI replies, passwords, authentication codes, payment numbers, medical records, crisis details, or other private or identifying information. A short description of the problem is enough to begin.

AI sharing permission in the iOS app

The native app asks before it sends a first message through Stabilize to OpenAI. To revoke that permission, open About, find AI sharing permission, and choose Revoke AI Sharing Permission. Revoking stops future messages until you allow sharing again; it does not recall messages already sent or reverse processing that has already occurred.

Privacy and deletion requests

Use the email address above and put “Privacy request” in the subject. Say whether you used the native iOS app, guest web chat, or signed-in web chat and give an approximate date and time, but do not paste the conversation. Native and web guest chats are not assigned to a Stabilize account, so support may be unable to identify a particular guest message. We will review requests for data Stabilize controls and explain when a service provider's own process or retention terms apply.

Safety boundary

Support email is not monitored as an emergency or crisis service. Do not wait for an email response when someone may be in immediate danger or needs urgent medical care. In the United States, call 911 for an emergency or call or text 988 for crisis support.
