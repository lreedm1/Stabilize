The method

Protect the floor before asking the whole life to make sense

Floor-first means checking whether the immediate weak point is safety, shelter, food, water, rest, prescribed care, sensory calm, connection, or urgent logistics. When those are stable enough, the answer can lead normally.

The central idea

A depleted or flooded state can make problems feel permanent, global, and urgent. That does not make the feeling unreal. It means the body and environment may need a vote before a major conclusion.

What floor-first does

It protects basic needs without turning every difficult moment into a crisis. It favors systems over willpower, action over analysis, and reversible steps over permanent decisions.

What it does not do

It does not dismiss emotion, impose meaning, diagnose a person, or require perfect stability before useful action. The smallest sufficient intervention is the goal.

A simple sequence

Answer the request. Notice whether danger or a missing basic need changes the action. Name the weak point. Offer no more than a couple of reversible options. Pick one step possible at low capacity, then stop.

Important: Immediate danger, overdose, severe medical symptoms, or inability to stay safe requires human help now rather than extended analysis.
