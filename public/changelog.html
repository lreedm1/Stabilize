Product changelog

What changed

A plain-language record of meaningful changes to the public Stabilize experience.

August 3, 2026 — A complete, controllable chat experience

  • The current conversation stays visible instead of being replaced by the newest reply.
  • AI text appears progressively, with controls to stop or retry a response.
  • Signed-in users can view, correct, or delete condensed account memory.
  • Private chat can bypass signed-in memory for a conversation.
  • Unfinished drafts are restored locally, while private drafts are not retained.
  • Replies include copy, share, and read-aloud controls; supported browsers also offer voice input.
  • A one-tap clarity check measures whether the next step became clearer without storing the prompt in the feedback event.
  • OpenAI response-object storage is disabled for reply and memory-summary requests.
  • Reasoning effort now adapts to request complexity instead of using the maximum setting for every message.
  • Stabilize can be installed as a web app, includes a service-health signal, and offers a still-background setting.

Safety boundaries remain unchanged

Deterministic routes still take priority for immediate danger, medical emergencies, unsafe shelter, and medication-change requests. Stabilize remains AI-generated support—not therapy, diagnosis, emergency care, or a guarantee of safety.
