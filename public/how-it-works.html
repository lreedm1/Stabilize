How it works

A smaller next step when everything feels large

Stabilize is a free AI check-in for adults in overloaded moments. It is designed to answer the immediate request, notice when a basic need or safety issue changes the action, and reduce the problem to one manageable step.

1. Describe what is happening

You can write naturally. A useful starting point is the problem, what feels urgent, and what you need help deciding or doing.

2. Protect the floor when needed

When safety, shelter, food, water, rest, medication access, connection, or urgent logistics are the weak point, Stabilize prioritizes that need before inviting a large conclusion.

3. Reduce cognitive load

The response aims to separate what is known from what is uncertain, name the likeliest failure point, and avoid unnecessary analysis.

4. Choose a reversible action

The goal is usually one action that remains possible at low capacity: a meal, a message, a call, a pause, a smaller task, or a way to preserve options.

Limits: Stabilize is not therapy, diagnosis, medical treatment, legal representation, or emergency response. In immediate danger or a medical emergency, contact a person or service that can respond now.
